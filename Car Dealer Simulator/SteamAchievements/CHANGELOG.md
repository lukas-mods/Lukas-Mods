## 1.0 - First Release

First public release. Steam Achievements adds an app to the tablet and the office computer that lists all 203 of the game's Steam achievements, and a small panel on the HUD that keeps your chosen ones in view.

- See every achievement with its icon, description and progress, grouped by tier, and how many Steam players have each one.
- Filters (All, Tracked, In Progress, Done) and a search box.
- Track up to 4 achievements on a HUD panel; with nothing tracked, it suggests the 4 easiest.
- Live progress: counts move the moment the game reports them.
- Hidden achievements stay hidden until unlocked.
- It only reads your progress; it never unlocks anything.
