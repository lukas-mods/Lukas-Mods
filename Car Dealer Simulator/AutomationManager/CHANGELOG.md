# Automation Manager — Changelog

Released versions only.

## 1.0.1 - Mechanic Fixes

Bug fixes from player reports. No new features.

- Replace Damaged now works: the mechanic gets the parts, fuses and fluids to change, picked from your storage the way the game's own Automation tab picks them. Before, the car went to the mechanic with nothing to do and came back untouched.
- Disassemble now takes the parts and fuses off and drains the fluids.
- Custom now changes fuses and fluids too, and fits parts that are missing. The windshield is replaced when it needs it.
- A car with nothing for the mechanic to do now skips the mechanic and gets the rest of its profile, instead of riding to the mechanic for nothing.
- Parts picked for a job that doesn't go ahead go back to storage.
- If a car's task is removed before the platform picks the car up (for example by another mod), the car is no longer treated as finished.

## 1.0 - First Release

First public release. Automation Manager adds an app to the tablet and the office computer that sets up your cars' automation tasks and shows the whole shop at a glance.

- Profiles: pick which stations a car visits, the paint colour and where it finishes. The mechanic step can Replace Damaged parts with one part type, use Custom (a part type for every slot, taken from your storage, all or nothing), or Disassemble the car. Sport and Race parts need the Up 2 You DLC.
- Give a car on a parking spot near the warehouse a profile, and it gets its task through the game's own Add Automation Task. Select Cars or Shift-click gives several cars the same profile.
- Board: your shop from above, with every station's Working, Idle or Reserved, each car where it is, and what the platform is doing and what's in its way. List shows the same cars as rows, and the Cars tab lists the cars waiting near the warehouse, each with the game's own photo.
- Unstick and Reset Car free the platform from a stuck car; every other car keeps its task and its parts. Lower Lift is offered for a mechanic's lift left up over an empty bay, and Free Station for a station the game still holds with no car in it.
- Car Pickup: the transporter's list and its Pick up when setting. Add Bought Cars fills the list for you, Send Now sends the transporter with what's on it, and the page shows when it's back.
- Front Parking: your Front Parking spots in one row, each with its car and whether it's listed.
- Post for Sale: a profile can price a car to a Competitiveness level and post it for you when the platform leaves it at Front Parking, through the game's own ad call, and the ad is read back to confirm it. The mod never takes an ad down.
- Report a Bug and Request a Feature buttons in the app's header, and Help for every page.
